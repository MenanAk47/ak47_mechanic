## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🚗 Advanced Customization & Modern NUI

* **Modern Tablet Interface:** Sleek, responsive web UI with smooth animations, intuitive categorization, and right-click camera pan and orbital control around the vehicle.
* **Complete Tuning Suite:** Full performance upgrades (Engine, Brakes, Transmission, Suspension, Armor, Turbo, Nitrous) and extensive cosmetic options (Spoilers, Bumpers, Skirts, Hoods, Exhausts, Grilles, Roofs, Fenders, Roll Cages).
* **Interior & Wheel Styling:** Custom racing seats, custom steering wheels, dashboard trims, dials, plaques, and an expansive catalog of wheel categories including Benny's Original and Open Wheel.
* **Gen9 Chameleon Respray & RGB:** Primary, secondary, pearlescent, wheel, dash, and interior colors with custom RGB sliders and GTA V Gen9 Chameleon paint ramp effects.
* **Interactive Vehicle Controls:** Toggle vehicle doors, hood, trunk, and test horn audio directly inside the customization menu.

### 🔧 Immersive Mechanic Roleplay & Usable Props

* **Interactive Mechanic Process:** Option to require mechanics to physically grab and install components rather than applying upgrades instantly from a menu.
* **Physical 3D Props:** Realistic props attached and carried by mechanics—including engine hoists, toolboxes, wheels, bumpers, hoods, exhausts, spoilers, and seats—with interactive pickup, carry, and drop commands.
* **Color-Synced Spray Particles:** Respraying vehicles generates realistic spray paint can animations and synchronized RGB paint smoke particles.
* **Usable Repair & Maintenance Kits:** Dedicated standalone items including Tire Repair Kits (with burst tire animation), Repair Kits, Advanced Repair Kits (engine, body, deformation, and fuel tank restoration), and Cleaning Kits with sponge props.

### 💼 Economy, Billing & Workshop Management

* **Dynamic Vehicle-Based Pricing:** Upgrade costs automatically scale as a percentage of the vehicle's dealership price, ensuring balanced pricing for low-end vs. supercar modifications.
* **Static Fallback & Multipliers:** Fully customizable static price tables with category multipliers when not using dealership pricing.
* **Itemized Invoicing System:** Mechanics can generate detailed bills sent directly to nearby customers to review subtotal, labor fees, and total costs before payment.
* **Society & Business Revenue:** Configurable labor fees and shop revenue cuts automatically deposited into company society accounts (ESX Society / qb-management / qbx_management).
* **Multi-Shop & Garage Integration:** Configure multiple mechanic locations (Bennys, LS Customs, custom shops) with dedicated vehicle garages, rank access, employee stashes, and parts supply stores.

### 🚛 Diagnostics, Towing & NPC Roadside Missions

* **Component Diagnostic Menu:** Detailed ox_lib / Lib47 context menu showing color-coded health percentages for body, engine, fuel tank, and individual wheels (requires a mechanic toolbox nearby to perform repairs).
* **Dual Towing Systems:** Scaleform-controlled flatbed and Guardian ramp towing with real-time height, movement, and tilt controls, plus a dynamic physics-based tow rope system with detach protection.
* **Dynamic NPC Service Calls:** Automated NPC roadside distress calls (`/mecnpcjob`) featuring stranded vehicles, repair and tow-to-depot objectives, and dynamic payouts based on distance and damage extent.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Extensive Inventory Compatibility:** Built-in support for ox_inventory, qb-inventory, qs-inventory, and ps-inventory with pre-configured items, SQL setups, and high-resolution inventory icons.

## ⚙️ How To Install:

* Download ak47_mechanic from your cfx portal.
* Add the script in your resources folder.
* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
